# Price Elasticity Analysis

A data-driven pricing optimization project focused on analyzing how price changes influence sales and demand across products. This project uses statistical modeling and business analysis to estimate price elasticity, identify product sensitivity, and support data-backed pricing decisions.

## Project Overview

The goal of this project was to understand the relationship between pricing and sales performance, and to help answer business questions such as:

- How sensitive are customers to price changes?
- Which products are price elastic vs. inelastic?
- How can pricing decisions improve revenue and demand planning?
- What role do external and operational factors play in sales behavior?

This project was designed to combine analytics, statistical modeling, and business decision-making in a practical pricing context.

## Business Problem

Businesses often apply broad pricing changes without knowing how different products respond. This can lead to lost revenue, lower sales volume, or missed opportunities for margin optimization.

This analysis was built to estimate price elasticity at a product/category level and provide insights that could support smarter pricing strategy, demand planning, and decision-making.

## Objectives

- Measure the impact of price on sales
- Estimate product-level or category-level price elasticity
- Identify products with elastic and inelastic demand
- Evaluate pricing patterns using regression-based models
- Translate model findings into actionable business recommendations

## Analytical Approach

The project involved the following steps:

1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation**
   - Cleaned pricing, sales, and inventory-related data
   - Handled missing values and inconsistent records
   - Prepared features for modeling and analysis

2. **Exploratory Data Analysis**
   - Examined price trends, sales patterns, and product-level variation
   - Identified relationships between price, sales, and other variables
   - Visualized data distributions and outliers

3. **Modeling**
   - Applied regression-based techniques to estimate price elasticity
   - Used models such as OLS and 2SLS to better understand price-sales relationships
   - Evaluated model outputs to interpret demand sensitivity

4. **Business Interpretation**
   - Classified products/categories based on elasticity behavior
   - Interpreted model results in business terms
   - Generated pricing recommendations based on sensitivity patterns

## Key Insights

- Price changes showed a measurable impact on sales across products
- Demand sensitivity varied by product/category, indicating that a single pricing strategy may not work for all products
- Some products behaved as relatively inelastic, suggesting pricing flexibility
- Other products showed higher sensitivity, requiring more cautious pricing changes
- The analysis highlighted how data can support pricing strategy instead of relying only on assumptions
